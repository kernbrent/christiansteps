Christian Steps Ministries — Hope Sojourns

Personally received gifts

Record the original donor first. Keep the person holding the funds and the later bank transfer separate.

Original donor and gift

Search Hope Sojourns contacts. If the donor is missing, add the contact in HS, then search again. HS contact access is required.

If there is no reference, save the gift and attach its original screenshot before sending to HS.

Gift reconciliation

Gifts − fees − approved expenses − cleared bank transfers = funds still held. These records and HS entries represent the same gifts; do not add them together as two donations.
