Personally received gifts for Hope Sojourns

This process follows each original donor's gift through Brent's personal payment account and bank account into the Christian Steps Ministries bank account, then into Hope Sojourns donor and financial records. The personal-gift workflow was released on September 23, 2026. Existing PayPal-only transfer controls must not be used to represent personal Venmo or Zelle payments.

1. Gather the original gift information

For each giver, save the original Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal transaction details or screenshot and the message identifying the intended ministry or purpose. Record the giver's name, original payment date, gross gift amount, payment service, transaction reference, and any processing fee. Keep the original gift date separate from the later bank deposit date. A personal gift to Brent must not be relabeled as somebody else's ministry donation merely because Brent later contributes that money.

2. Enter the original donor in Christian Steps MMT

In the Personally received gifts section, open Original donor and gift. Create one gift record per original donor payment before recording the bank transfer.

Select the original donor from the Hope Sojourns contact list. For example, select your mother's own contact for her gift and your neighbor's own contact for the neighbor's gift. If the person is missing, create or review that contact in Hope Sojourns first, then return and select it. Matching names alone must not automatically combine contacts.

Enter:

  • Original donor: the selected contact, with name and available contact details shown for confirmation.
  • Received by: Brent Kern, as the person holding the ministry funds.
  • Method: Personal Venmo, Personal Zelle, or Personal PayPal.
  • Original date, gift amount, transaction ID or supporting screenshot, and any fee.
  • Designation: Christian Steps Ministries — Hope Sojourns, with a specific purpose or trip when applicable.
  • Supporting note establishing the intended ministry gift.

Choose Save original gift. It starts as Awaiting transfer. Brent's role as recipient or transfer agent must be stored separately from the original donor. The name on the later bank deposit must not replace this donor selection.

3. Move the money to your personal bank account if necessary

If funds remain in Venmo or PayPal, withdraw them to your personal bank account and retain that withdrawal reference. If the payment is already in your bank account, as may be the case with Zelle, skip this step. This movement is part of the same gift's trail, not a new donation.

Keep gross gifts, processing fees, and cash received separate. Record fees with evidence rather than reducing the amount credited to the original donor without explanation.

4. Transfer the ministry funds to the CSM bank account

Use the bank's ordinary transfer or deposit process to move the available ministry funds to the Christian Steps Ministries bank account. Retain the confirmation. Use a memo such as 'Hope Sojourns gifts held by Brent — batch [reference]'. Avoid putting private donor information in a publicly visible memo.

Several gifts may be included in one deposit. Keep an allocation showing how much of that deposit belongs to each original gift. Do not record the deposit as a new donation from Brent.

5. Record and reconcile the CSM bank deposit

In the CSM personal-gift record, use Record cleared transfer, expense, or fee and choose Cleared transfer into CSM bank. Enter the actual transfer date, amount, bank reference, and confirmation. Link it to the original gift records and allocate the transferred amount across them. Enter the bank cleared date and choose Record settlement only after the deposit clears; initiating a transfer is not the same as confirming receipt.

Allow partial transfers and show the remaining amount held. If the bank transaction was already imported or entered elsewhere in MMT, link or reconcile that existing transaction instead of adding another income entry. The transfer is a movement of the already recorded funds, not another charitable gift.

6. Receive the designated gift in Hope Sojourns MMT

After the source information and CSM bank receipt have been reviewed, use Send to Hope Sojourns. This sends the gift record for review; it does not move money between banks.

Open Hope Sojourns MMT's Payment inbox. Review the original donor, original gift date, gross amount, fees, designation, and linked transfer. Confirm the existing donor contact and approve once. The resulting entry must credit that original donor. Brent remains visible only as the person who received and transferred the funds. Retrying a send or approval must not create a second gift.

CSM and Hope Sojourns records represent the same underlying contribution. A combined ministry report must not add both representations together as two gifts. Nothing in this workflow is sent to Josh Beyond Borders.

7. Document any expenses paid from the held funds

The preferred straightforward trail is to transfer the funds to CSM and pay ministry expenses through the ministry's normal process. If an approved ministry expense was already paid directly from the held funds, record its date, vendor, amount, ministry purpose, and receipt separately and link it to the relevant gifts. Do not treat an expense as a bank transfer or credit the holder as a donor. Do not record a second expense if it already exists elsewhere in MMT.

The reconciliation must show: original gifts minus documented fees, approved expenses paid from those funds, and transfers into CSM equals funds still held. A gift settled partly through approved expenses must not misleadingly say that its full amount reached the bank.

8. Review acknowledgments and year-end statements

Review the donor attribution and supporting evidence before issuing an acknowledgment. Use Christian Steps Ministries — Hope Sojourns as the ministry identification and credit the original giver. Preserve both original receipt and bank-clearance dates for the ministry's review. A transfer confirmation alone is not the donor acknowledgment.

For a written acknowledgment, include the ministry name, cash contribution amount, and the applicable statement concerning goods or services provided. The ministry must verify the appropriate wording and acknowledgment date. IRS reference: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contributions-written-acknowledgments

Example with illustrative amounts

Your mother gives $100 through personal Venmo and your neighbor gives $150 through personal Zelle. Enter two original gift records, linked to their two contacts. With no fees or expenses, a single $250 deposit into the CSM bank can be allocated $100 and $150 to those records. The deposit identifies Brent as the transfer agent. Donor statements credit your mother $100 and your neighbor $150; Brent receives no donor credit for passing along their money. The remaining amount held is $0.

Finding the screen and correcting mistakes

In CSM MMT, choose Personally received gifts from Giving or the Ledger navigation. Choose Email, Name, or Phone number, enter the search text, then select the matching HS contact. Email is selected initially. Only the selected field is searched; phone searches ignore common formatting. The search requires shared sign-in, CSM giving access, and HS contact access. It never makes the holder the donor automatically.

Open a saved gift to attach evidence, record settlements, review audit history, and send it to HS. The default designation is Hope Sojourns; a purpose or trip is descriptive in this version and does not apply money to a traveler's account automatically. Use Download reconciliation CSV for the original donor, holder, bank references, settlement details, and amount still held.

Before sending, reverse an incorrect settlement with a reason. Void an incorrect gift after reversing any settlements, then enter its replacement using a distinct reference noting the voided gift. Voided and reversed entries remain visible for audit. After a gift has been sent, the source and its evidence are locked; coordinate any correction with the HS reviewer rather than re-entering a duplicate. A denied gift remains in history and is excluded from giving statements.

Choose Refresh HS status / retry delivery if the connection fails or an approval has not appeared yet. The same gift is resent safely; it is not recreated. Personal gifts appear in CSM giving letters only after HS approval. HS giving statements also retain the original giver. Use one reviewed official acknowledgment for the underlying gift; reports from both portals are not separate contributions.

The dedicated CSM reconciliation is the source for this personal-funds trail. It does not create fake PayPal transactions or automatically insert a duplicate bank-income row into CSM's general bookkeeping ledger. If a bank deposit was imported there, use its reference in the settlement and have the bookkeeper classify it as the transfer of already recorded funds. Any listed expense is created once in HS on approval; do not separately enter the same expense in HS first. If that expense already exists in HS, resolve it with the bookkeeper before using this settlement workflow.
