A shared ministry

Your gift supports meaningful partnership.

Giving helps Christian Steps Ministries and Hope Sojourns follow the priorities of local leaders, contribute to work already underway, and invite people into a deeper life of faith and service.

Service begins with humility

Hope Sojourns brings willing people alongside trusted local ministries. These journeys create room to listen and learn before acting, to meet practical needs with respect, and to build relationships that extend beyond a single trip.

Support is not limited to one destination or one journey. It strengthens the broader ministry work of Christian Steps Ministries and Hope Sojourns.
