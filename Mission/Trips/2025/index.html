2025: Learning to See People in Athens

Athens reshaped our understanding of service. Local partners, neighbors, and ordinary encounters taught us that humility begins with listening and dignity begins with truly seeing a person.

A journey that changed our posture

Our team served alongside churches and local ministries caring for refugees, people experiencing homelessness, and vulnerable neighbors. Time with Sofia and Nikos of Hellenic Ministries of Athens and a gathering at the Albanian Church of Athens helped us understand the importance of serving through trusted relationships.

A simple request for a red juice box became one of the year’s most enduring lessons: people are never projects or tasks to complete. Every person has preferences, a story, and God-given dignity. We came home determined to listen more carefully and serve without assuming we already knew what another person needed.

Visits to the Acropolis, Mars Hill, the original Olympic Stadium, the Corinth Canal, Ancient Corinth, and Aegina Island connected the work of the present with the history and faith of the region.
