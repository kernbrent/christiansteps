Athens, Greece · 2025

Serving with Humility in Athens

We came ready to help, but the people we met taught us that meaningful service begins with listening, honoring each person, and working beside those who already know and love their community.

Alongside Local Ministries

Our team served alongside churches and ministry partners caring for refugees, people experiencing homelessness, and other vulnerable neighbors throughout Athens. We helped with practical needs, shared food and essential items, prayed, listened, and made room for conversations that reminded us that every person deserves to be seen.

We also spent time with Sofia and Nikos of Hellenic Ministries of Athens. Their local knowledge and steady commitment helped our visiting team understand the value of relationship-centered ministry. A gathering at the Albanian Church of Athens gave us another meaningful opportunity to worship and connect with people serving faithfully in the city.

She Wanted a Red One

During homeless outreach, a woman asked for a red juice box. We had a blue one available, and at first the difference seemed unimportant. Then a teammate quietly reminded us, “She wanted a red one.”

That small request carried a lasting lesson. People are not projects, problems, or tasks to complete. They have preferences, stories, and God-given dignity. Service is not simply giving what is convenient; it is slowing down long enough to notice the person in front of us.

Learning from Place and History

Beyond our ministry work, we experienced the history and beauty surrounding Athens. Visits to the Acropolis, Hadrian’s Arch, the original Olympic Stadium in Athens, the Corinth Canal, and Ancient Corinth gave us space to reflect on Scripture, history, and the generations of people who have lived and worshiped in this region.

We returned home grateful for the partners who welcomed us, the neighbors who trusted us with their stories, and the ways God used ordinary encounters to deepen our understanding of humble service.
