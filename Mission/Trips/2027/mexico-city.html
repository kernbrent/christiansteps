Joint ministry opportunity

Serving neighbors experiencing homelessness

Christian Steps Ministries and Hope Sojourns are developing a 2027 mission trip to Mexico City in partnership with Metro Relief of Dallas. The team will support a sister ministry already serving people experiencing homelessness through consistent, local relationships.

The goal is not to arrive with a preset program. We will listen to ministry leaders, learn how they serve, and contribute where our presence is most useful. Final activities will be shaped by the sister ministry’s priorities and the realities of its ongoing work.

The partnership

Local leadership, shared service

Metro Relief of Dallas will help coordinate the joint team and connect us with its sister ministry in Mexico City. Local leaders will guide the ministry schedule, outreach approach, and practical assignments.

The team

10–15 prepared volunteers

The targeted team size is 10 to 15 people. We will look for participants who are flexible, culturally humble, comfortable serving as part of a team, and ready to treat every person with dignity.

How we may serve

Practical care built around relationship

Potential activities may include street outreach, meal or supply distribution, prayer, listening, conversation, and other practical work requested by the host ministry. Specific responsibilities, preparation, and safety guidance will be confirmed before registration opens.

Planning status: Dates, cost, travel details, host-ministry information, and final assignments are still being developed.
