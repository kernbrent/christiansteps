Planning the year ahead

2027: Opportunities Taking Shape

Four developing journeys invite teams to serve in very different settings. Dates, costs, team roles, and final projects will be confirmed with local partners as each opportunity develops.

Preparing before we go

Our goal for 2027 is not simply to send more teams. It is to prepare the right teams for work that local partners have identified as useful. Each journey will be shaped by listening, clear expectations, cultural humility, and practical preparation.

Shepherd of the Ozarks offers an opportunity for hands-on service close to home. Athens continues relationships with ministries serving people experiencing homelessness, refugees, and people vulnerable to trafficking. Nice introduces an opportunity to help create welcoming Bible experiences for children and families through International VBS Ministries. Mexico City adds a joint opportunity with Metro Relief of Dallas to support a sister ministry serving neighbors experiencing homelessness.

These pages are planning summaries. Information will be updated as conversations with partners clarify the work, travel schedule, team size, and skills needed.
