Year in review

2026: Deepening Partnerships and Preparing the Way

Two very different journeys shared one purpose: listen to local leaders, strengthen relationships, and prepare people to serve in ways that are thoughtful, practical, and sustainable.

Returning and looking ahead

In Athens, a twelve-person team from Texas, Colorado, and Wisconsin served alongside local ministries caring for people experiencing homelessness, refugees, and neighbors vulnerable to trafficking. The return journey built on relationships already established and invited the team to serve with humility, offer practical help, and make room for genuine human connection.

Later in the year, Christian Steps Ministries visited Shepherd of the Ozarks in Arkansas. This was a listening and planning journey: walking the property, meeting the people who serve there, identifying practical needs, and learning how future Hope Sojourns teams could arrive prepared to help.

Together, these experiences reinforced a central conviction. Good mission work is not measured only by activity. It grows from trust, preparation, cultural humility, and a willingness to follow the priorities of people already serving their communities.
