A Planning Visit to Shepherd of the Ozarks

Christian Steps Ministries traveled to Shepherd of the Ozarks (SOTO) in Arkansas for a four-day visit centered on listening, learning, prayer, and preparation. The trip gave us an opportunity to see the ministry firsthand, meet the people who serve there, and better understand the work, facilities, and practical needs of this special place.

This visit was used to plan how and when HopeSojourns Ministry can begin sending work teams to Shepherd of the Ozarks. By walking the grounds, discussing possible projects, and considering the gifts future volunteers can bring, we began laying the groundwork for meaningful service trips that are helpful, well prepared, and responsive to SOTO’s actual needs.

Photo & Video Gallery Trip Devotional

Preparing the Way to Serve

Planning is an important part of faithful service. Before teams arrive with tools and willing hands, relationships must be built, needs must be understood, and the right work must be identified. This trip helped HopeSojourns take those first steps with care and humility.

The days at SOTO offered time to observe the property, talk through future possibilities, and imagine how work teams might support the ministry in the seasons ahead. The goal is not simply to complete projects, but to create opportunities for people to serve side by side, encourage one another, and participate in work that strengthens SOTO’s mission.

What We Came to Learn

  • Which projects would be most useful to SOTO
  • What skills, tools, and team sizes future trips may require
  • When work teams can serve most effectively
  • How HopeSojourns can prepare volunteers well

Looking Ahead

The visit marked the beginning of a new chapter. As plans develop, Christian Steps Ministries and HopeSojourns will continue seeking God’s direction for teams that arrive ready to work, ready to listen, and ready to serve.
