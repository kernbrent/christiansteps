SOTO 2026 Devotional

Preparing the Way

Scripture: “Commit to the Lord whatever you do, and he will establish your plans.” — Proverbs 16:3

Trip Summary Gallery

Mission work is often pictured in the moment of action: a hammer striking a nail, a wall receiving fresh paint, a meal being served, or a prayer being offered. Yet faithful service begins long before the work team arrives. It begins with listening.

Our visit to Shepherd of the Ozarks was a trip of preparation. We walked, watched, asked questions, and considered how HopeSojourns Ministry might send future teams to serve well. There is humility in admitting that enthusiasm alone is not enough. Love takes time to understand what is truly needed. Stewardship asks us to plan carefully so that willing hands become a genuine blessing.

Proverbs reminds us to commit our work to the Lord. That commitment is more than asking God to approve plans we have already made. It means placing our ideas, timing, abilities, and expectations before Him. It means remaining open when His direction differs from ours.

Jesus also taught His followers to count the cost before building (Luke 14:28). Planning is not a lack of faith; it can be an expression of faithfulness. When we prepare thoughtfully, we honor the people we hope to serve, the volunteers who will give their time, and the God whose name our service bears.

The July visit planted seeds. Some future results may be visible in repaired buildings or completed projects. Others may appear in relationships, encouragement, and lives changed through serving together. Our calling now is to keep praying, keep listening, and take the next faithful step when God opens the way.

Reflection

  1. Where might God be asking me to listen before I act?
  2. How can careful preparation become an act of love?
  3. What plan do I need to commit to the Lord today?

Prayer

Lord, thank You for every opportunity to serve. Give us humble hearts that listen before acting, wisdom to prepare well, and patience to follow Your timing. Bless Shepherd of the Ozarks and all who serve there. Guide HopeSojourns as future work teams are planned, and shape each volunteer into a faithful servant who brings encouragement as well as willing hands. Establish the work that honors You, and help us trust You with every next step. Amen.
