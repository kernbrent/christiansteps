2018: Serving and Learning in Kenya

The 2018 journey combined practical work, faith sharing, new friendships, and the wonder of experiencing Kenya’s people, communities, and landscape.

A year shaped by willing hands

Christian Steps Ministries traveled through Nairobi, Maua, Samburu, Maasai Mara, and Lake Nakuru. In Maua, the team shared devotionals, spent time with local children, and worked alongside partners on a hospital boundary wall designed to improve safety for patients and staff.

The journey reminded us that mission work includes both practical service and relationship. Construction mattered, but so did laughter with children, encouragement shared across cultures, and the humility to receive the welcome of local communities.

Travel through Kenya also offered time to witness the beauty and variety of creation. The team returned home grateful for every person who welcomed us and for the many ways service, learning, and worship became part of the same experience.
