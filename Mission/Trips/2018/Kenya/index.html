Kenya · 2018

Serving, Learning, and Seeing God’s World in Kenya

Our journey carried us through Nairobi, Maua, Samburu, Maasai Mara, and Lake Nakuru—places that invited us to serve with our hands, share our faith, build relationships, and experience the beauty of Kenya.

Faith Made Practical

In Maua, our ministry took several forms. We taught devotionals and shared encouragement, trusting that God can use simple words offered faithfully. We also worked alongside local partners at a hospital compound, helping build a new perimeter wall designed to keep patients and staff safe by limiting access to people who were not permitted on the grounds.

The work was physical, but its purpose was deeply personal. Every stone and section of wall represented greater safety for people seeking care and for those serving them. The project reminded us that ministry often looks like meeting a practical need with willing hands.

Relationships Beyond the Work

Some of the most meaningful moments came through meeting and playing with local children. Laughter, games, conversation, and time spent together crossed cultural and language differences. Those encounters reminded us that mission work is not only about completing a task; it is also about noticing people, receiving their welcome, and sharing joy.

Across Kenya

Our travels also took us to Nairobi, Samburu, Maasai Mara, and Lake Nakuru. Along the way, several safaris gave us the opportunity to see giraffes, elephants, lions, antelope, ostriches, warthogs, hyenas, and great herds moving across the landscape. The scale and variety of creation gave the journey another kind of devotional—one written in open skies, grasslands, wildlife, and wonder.

Kenya 2018 brought together teaching, construction, friendship, cultural learning, and rest. We returned home grateful for the people who welcomed us, the work we were allowed to share, and the many ways God met us throughout the journey.
